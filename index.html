The Platform

Our Data Marketing (DM) platform is calibrated for digital marketing professionals to enhance their understanding and achieve better results in media buying activities. It unifies cross‑device signals, partner data and geolocation intelligence.

Built for marketers, it makes audience creation, segmentation and activation simple while scaling effortlessly from one‑off bursts to enterprise campaigns.

With access to over 3.5 billion devices and more than 25,000 audience segments, our device graph provides unparalleled reach and granularity.

Powered by AI‑driven insights, Tekma activates your audiences across major DSPs, ensuring your message reaches the right people wherever they are.

Data Collection

We collect data from all available sources including mobile devices, desktops and private databases, using innovative methods to track varying predefined attributes from geographics to demographics, psychographics and more.

Data Processing

Segmentation is the core of our data processing capabilities, enabling us to offer a wide spectrum of analysis from base operators to recency & frequency at segment level, constraint conditions and other complex settings.

Our solutions are compliant with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) applicable in all European Union countries.

AI & Data Edge

Tekma’s AI engine delivers real‑time analysis, customizable models and rapid prediction of user behaviour. Our algorithms ingest billions of signals every day to forecast customer actions and optimise campaigns, reducing guesswork and enabling smarter decisions. By using mobile advertising IDs instead of cookies, Tekma achieves cross‑device recognition accuracy in the 60–90 % range while complying with GDPR and IAB standards. Massive datasets power the creation of precise audiences and actionable recommendations.
