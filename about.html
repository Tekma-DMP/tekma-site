About Us

At TEKMA, we believe that in today’s data-driven world, reaching the right people at the right time matters more than ever.

Harnessing our advanced data platform and programmatic ecosystem, we empower brands and agencies to deliver precision advertising in premium publisher environments - across mobile, social, apps, SDKs, CTV, and programmatic channels.

Who We Are

TEKMA is a next-generation Data Management Platform (DMP) built for the complexity of modern advertising.

Our team unites experts in data science and AdTech, all driven by a single mission: turning audience signals into meaningful results.

What We Do

We collect, combine, and activate data from diverse sources. From first-party datasets to enriched audience segments, we transform raw signals into privacy-safe, high-impact audience profiles.

These audiences are then activated through direct integrations with leading programmatic channels, enabling advertisers to engage consumers with precision across mobile, social, and connected TV.

Why It Matters

In an era of fragmented screens, shifting behaviors, and rising consumer expectations, advertisers face a clear challenge:
